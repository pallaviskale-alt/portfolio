PROJECT 05

Marketing Data Integration & Automation

An integrated marketing data and media buying reporting capability that brings campaign, platform and buying information into a more consistent view—helping teams track activity, review delivery and reduce manual reporting effort.

MarTech, Media Buying & Automation

Business challenge

Media and marketing teams work with information spread across buying plans, campaign delivery reports, advertising platforms and analytics sources. Consolidating these inputs can be time-consuming and makes it harder to maintain a consistent view of buying activity and campaign performance.

Solution scope

  • Bring relevant media buying, campaign and marketing-platform data together to support consolidated reporting.
  • Structure key buying and delivery indicators so teams can review planned activity, actual delivery and campaign status in a common view.
  • Use API-led data movement and repeatable data workflows, where applicable, to reduce manual data collation and reporting tasks.
  • Support downstream dashboards, stakeholder reviews and more timely performance discussions.

Technology & business relevance

The wider portfolio includes work with Google Ads, Meta, DV360, GA4 and CRM-related data, alongside cloud data and reporting components such as BigQuery, Azure SQL, Azure Data Factory, Databricks and Power BI across different workflows. The specific tools and measures used in the video should be read from the demonstrated solution rather than assumed to apply to every integration.

Impact areas

Improved visibility into media buying and campaign delivery, more consistent reporting, reduced manual effort and better-informed stakeholder decisions. Reported outcomes across selected initiatives included approximately 40% less manual effort and potential capacity savings of 2–3 FTE; these figures are portfolio-level examples and are not attributed to this video alone.
