PROJECT 07

Net Working Capital (NWC) Optimisation

A working-capital optimisation solution focused on improving cash utilisation by aligning the timing of client collections and creditor payments—helping increase favourable cash float and reduce funding exposure.

Finance Transformation & Automation

Business challenge

Timing differences between client collections and creditor payments can create avoidable funding exposure and missed opportunities to earn interest on available cash. The NWC tool supports better visibility and timing decisions across these working-capital flows.

Solution value

  • Optimises the timing of client collections to improve favourable cash float.
  • Supports more effective creditor-payment timing and reduces the number of days funding is required.
  • Helps improve working-capital utilisation through incremental interest earnings and reduced financing costs.
  • Provides a scalable financial-benefit opportunity as transaction volumes and working-capital efficiency improve.

PROJECT 08

TDS Reconciliation Engine & Automation

An end-to-end TDS reconciliation solution that automates client remittance advice processing, TDS accounting and quarterly reconciliation against Form 26AS—reducing manual effort while strengthening tax-credit realisation and financial-record accuracy.

Finance Automation & Reconciliation

Business challenge

Manual processing of client remittance advice, TDS accounting and periodic reconciliation can consume significant operational capacity. Identifying mismatches and unclaimed TDS credits also becomes more difficult as transaction volumes increase, potentially creating backlogs and delaying tax-credit realisation.

Solution capabilities

  • Automates end-to-end processing of client remittance advice and related TDS accounting.
  • Performs quarterly reconciliation of TDS records against Form 26AS.
  • Identifies reconciliation mismatches and unclaimed TDS credits for timely follow-up.
  • Reduces manual intervention and helps improve the accuracy, completeness and auditability of financial records.
